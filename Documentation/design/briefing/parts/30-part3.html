07Data, and when a fact became knowable

Fetching is a runtime of its own, 21 tools that get the bytes and publish them as references every other runtime reads. Before anything is fetched, a source can be asked what it can promise about when its facts became knowable.

Providers

A provider-agnostic interface serves OHLCV, fundamentals and metadata, with a free default and adapters for Polygon, Bloomberg and Databento. Databento is the one here that serves L2 depth and order-by-order events, and its key is read from the vendor's own variable, never passed through a spec, so it cannot be persisted into a model's lineage or a decision record. preflight_vendor_request says what a metered request would cost, and whether the data is there, before it is made.

Infrastructure

Retries with exponential backoff that never retry a caller's own mistake, a TTL and Parquet cache, and quality checks for missing bars, stale prices and price jumps. get_dataset_metadata reports what the provider guarantees: adjustment, survivorship, point-in-time values and the timezone.

The temporal contract

A quarterly filing describes 30 September and is published on 25 October. Joined on the quarter end it puts three weeks of hindsight into every row. describe_temporal_contract answers what a source can say about timing before anything is fetched, and a join the contract cannot support is refused rather than degraded silently. infer_temporal_contract does the same for a frame this library did not fetch.

Bundles and comparisons

A bundle holds several frames and the contract each was fetched under, validated as a unit. compare_ratio_frames classifies two providers' disagreement: a unit mismatch can be rescaled, a definition difference cannot.

Data too large to fetch

register_external_dataset makes a Parquet or CSV dataset already on disk resolvable as an sqt:// reference without copying it, and prepare_vendor_extract converts a raw vendor extract into the library's contract first. Continuous futures series, tick tapes, quote panels and order-book snapshots are published the same way.

08Research and statistical inference

The research runtime, 46 tools, describes an asset or a universe: its risk and technicals, and its statistical structure. It does not run strategies. Around it sits a family of error bars and tests, so a summary statistic arrives with how far it can be trusted.

A factor model, and which factors belong in it

estimate_factor_model fits loadings, a factor covariance and specific variances for many assets at once, with standard errors, and can publish B F B' + D as a covariance reference. screen_factor_candidates decides which candidate factors earn a place, judged out of sample: incremental R-squared against circularly shifted copies (Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted across candidates), stable loadings, a low VIF, and a minimum-variance book that is less volatile with the factor than without.

VaR as a forecast that can be checked

forecast_var_series makes a one-step-ahead VaR and expected shortfall for every date from the returns before it, by six methods: historical, normal, Cornish-Fisher, EWMA, filtered historical, and EVT on GARCH. backtest_var_forecasts tests the series against what followed: Kupiec, Christoffersen, conditional coverage, the Basel traffic light, and Acerbi-Szekely for ES. A model's own quantile column can be tested the same way, entity by entity, with overlapping multi-day outcomes thinned.

09Modeling

A separate 38-tool runtime builds, validates and scores statistical models as one ordered pipeline: dataset, leakage checks, walk-forward fitting, the registry, and evaluation of out-of-sample predictions. A feature lab of 11 more tools interrogates features before anything is fitted.

What it is built from

31 features, 27 estimators (19 always available and 8 that register only when their library is installed), 10 preprocessing steps and 18 target kinds, six of them buildable from prices. A feature matrix computed elsewhere can be registered without copying it.

Leakage, purged by date

Training rows are purged by each row's label end date, not by an integer offset: a horizon counts an entity's own bars, and on a sparse calendar an offset under-purges exactly where it matters. Point-in-time joins, check_leakage and a gate on each model's training information cutoff close the rest.

A registry with a lifecycle

Models are content-addressed, with signed manifests: attest_model_package says whether a package is still the one registered and whether a trusted key signed it. Promotion through lifecycle stages, drift monitoring and a bridge from predictions to a tradeable signal complete it.

Predictions into portfolios

evaluate_model_portfolio turns out-of-sample predictions into a simulated book net of costs. The mean_variance transform sizes it with the optimizer, trading from the book actually held after prices drift.

What the optimizer-sized transform was measured to do

On planted data, net of costs: with a real edge it ties rank weighting damped to half turnover (+0.01 ± 0.05 Sharpe). Where correlated names score alike it wins (+0.14 ± 0.07). With no edge it loses 0.26% a year, against 2.9% for damped rank. Trading from the drifted book added 0.02–0.03 Sharpe and cut no-edge turnover by a third.

10Backtesting, and how much of it is real

The backtest runtime, 35 tools, runs, optimizes and validates strategies, the built-in ones or a signal the caller computed. Eleven of them exist to answer a question a backtest cannot answer about itself: how much of the result is real.

Running

A vectorized engine with realistic costs, built-in strategies and parameter grids, a signal-panel backtester that accepts the caller's own matrix, pairs and futures backtests, walk-forward runs, Monte Carlo, and a shared-cash portfolio simulator with pluggable cost and constraint models.

Doubting

Deflated Sharpe at the number of trials, the probability of backtest overfitting, purged combinatorial cross-validation splits, the reality check, comparison against random entries, trade clustering, parameter decay, regime-stratified performance and the break-even cost a strategy can bear.

A Sharpe ratio is a claim about one path. The trial count, the cost it survives and the paths it did not take are what decide whether to believe it.

11Portfolio, allocation and finance

Three runtimes turn a view into capital. Portfolio (23 tools) builds and costs positions; allocation (10) splits capital across return streams; finance (9) prices what holding a book costs and what a stream of cash is worth.

The rebalance, net of trading costs

optimize_rebalance finds the trade to make from the weights held, with linear and quadratic costs in the objective. Names inside their no-trade band keep their weight exactly, and the frictionless target is reported beside the trade, so the gap shows what the costs held back. Two hundred assets solve in about 25 ms.

Choosing among rules as you go

select_allocation_rule picks at each rebalance from each rule's own out-of-sample record before that date, and reports the hindsight pick beside it. On planted data it kept 0.28 of a 0.37 Sharpe edge, cost 0.02 against equal weight with no edge, and never claimed one. An optional regime condition added 0.050 Sharpe to a volatility-targeted risk parity rule, ahead in 28 of 30 histories.

12Derivatives, delta one and microstructure

Three runtimes answer what an instrument is worth, which instrument is the cheapest way to hold an exposure, and what the market will charge to trade it.

Derivatives · 13

Pricing and greeks to second order, implied volatility, multi-leg payoffs, smile and term-structure fits with arbitrage checks, put-call parity, the expected move, delta-hedge simulation, full revaluation grids, and an option book's dollar greeks with the multiplier required. Quotes are arguments: the library fetches no option chains.

Delta one · 18

Carry and basis against a quoted future, curves and roll costs, beta translated into contracts and whether the hedge worked, baskets and index rebalances, ETF fair value, total return swaps and futures. The holding horizon can reverse which instrument is cheapest.

Microstructure · 17

Four tools measure spreads and order flow from ticks and refuse without a tick feed; seven estimate the same quantities from OHLCV, each saying what it is a proxy for. check_spread_proxy turns a proxy's error on one name into a number.
